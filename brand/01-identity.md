# koodalanne — Brand Identity

> Part 1 of the koodalanne Brand Bible. This is the strategy layer. Everything
> visual (tokens, components, assets) exists to express what is written here.
>
> **Name styling:** the name is always written **koodalanne** — all lowercase,
> including the first letter — in the wordmark and in running text.

---

## Brand statement

koodalanne is an independent software consultancy helping companies design, build
and evolve modern software. Combining senior engineering expertise with technical
leadership and product thinking, koodalanne bridges the gap between business,
people and code.

_Notice what's missing: no React, no TypeScript, no frameworks. Frameworks change.
Identity shouldn't._

## Mission

**Build software that companies can confidently grow on.**

## Vision

**Become the consultancy companies call when they need someone who can own problems
instead of just writing code.**

---

## Positioning

Not "Senior Developer." **Independent Software Consultant.**

Supporting pillars:

```
Engineering  ·  Technical Leadership  ·  Architecture
AI-Accelerated Delivery  ·  Product Collaboration
```

The differentiator: koodalanne owns the whole software lifecycle.

```
Business → Product → Architecture → Development → Delivery → Clients
```

A CTO hiring koodalanne should immediately think: **"He can own this."**

## Brand voice

Professional · Confident · Optimistic · Approachable · Never arrogant.
No corporate buzzwords. No "disrupting industries."

**Instead of:**
> We build scalable innovative solutions leveraging cutting-edge technology.

**Say:**
> Software should make businesses simpler, not more complicated.
>
> I help teams move confidently from ideas to production.

The three-beat rhythm: **Here's the problem. Here's the solution. Let's build it.**

---

## Target audience

Engineering Managers · CTOs · Heads of Engineering · Product Managers ·
Startup founders · Consultancies needing senior reinforcement.

## Core creative idea

> **Modern engineering with neon soul.**

Alternatives on brand: _"Engineering with a neon heartbeat."_ ·
_"Built with precision. Finished with personality."_

None of them mention the 80s. The feeling comes from visuals, not words.

---

## The feeling we're after

People should say **Premium · Confident · Memorable · Different** first —
and only after a minute realize _"…wait, these colors, the grid, the sunset —
this is Vice City."_ That moment of discovery is the brand.
